Why the thread?

The red thread is how I think about connected work.

A website, an ad and a social post may look different, but they still need to feel like they come from the same business.

The thread is a reminder to keep the message, visuals and experience connected. It also reflects how I work: I research, test, adjust and keep going until the result feels clear.

How I work

My process is practical, flexible and hands-on.

I ask questions, make a useful first version and improve it with feedback and real information. That approach has helped me manage projects even when I was the only person handling the work.
