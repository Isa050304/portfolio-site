Three ideas that became something real.

Each project started differently, but all of them required research, problem solving, and one final detail that made the idea feel complete.

Thread 01

Poster Design · Visual Storytelling

A football reimagined as a world of its own, with two people playing at its centre. The poster explores how sport can create connection beyond borders and differences.

Selected for an international competition and exhibited in Mexico.
