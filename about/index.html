About Me

I like ideas that get clearer the more you look at them.

I am a multidisciplinary designer based in Vancouver. I work across graphic design, editorial, digital experiences and visual storytelling, depending on what the idea needs.

The red thread connects a few things that feel personal to me: my curly hair, the idea of fate, knots, detours and the fact that design almost never moves in a perfectly straight line.

I care about work that feels thoughtful, useful and human. I like strong concepts, careful typography and details that reward a second look. I am especially interested in the moment when something starts feeling like its own little world.
